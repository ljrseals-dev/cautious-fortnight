# Credits

Ideas and prior work this build draws on. Entries marked **from memory** were not re-checked against the source and should be verified before being cited.

- Dudley, H. (1939/1940). The Vocoder / The Carrier Nature of Speech. Bell Labs. Channel vocoder principle: band envelopes drive a separate excitation.
- Glasberg, B. R., and Moore, B. C. J. (1990). Derivation of auditory filter shapes from notched-noise data. Hearing Research 47. ERB-rate band spacing. **From memory.**
- Peterson, G. E., and Barney, H. L. (1952). Control methods used in a study of the vowels. JASA 24. Typical formant differences between men, women and children, which motivate the size axis. **From memory.**
- Uncanny Voice (earlier build in this series): the list of cues (jitter, vibrato, formant wander, breath).

Code is original to this project. Size ratios and cue amounts are the author's choices, not from the cited papers.
