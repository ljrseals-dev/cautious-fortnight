Voice Morph

Change how big a voice sounds without changing what it says. Formants set the size of the throat and mouth; pitch sets how high the voice sings. Move them together for child to giant, or apart for stranger voices.

Human cues

Small imperfections that make a morphed voice read as a person, or deliberately as something close to one.

How it works

The voice is analyzed in narrow frequency bands. Each band's loudness over time is kept. The morphed voice rebuilds that loudness pattern through the same bands moved up or down in frequency, driven by a buzz at the pitch you chose. Moving the bands moves the formants; the buzz sets the pitch.

This is the channel vocoder idea from Dudley, 1940, used for formant warping.

Checked and not checked

Checked in headless Chromium: formant shift moves the spectral envelope by the set amount while the pitch holds; pitch shift moves the pitch while the envelope holds; the cues add drift, vibrato and wander that are absent when switched off; renders repeat exactly.

Not checked: how it sounds, whether words stay understandable, or how natural it feels. Band resolution limits the warp: vowels may smear, and fricatives move with the warp. Nobody has listened to it.
